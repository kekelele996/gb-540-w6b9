## Design Context

### Users

Cadastral surveyors, GIS analysts, reviewers, and auditors working at a desk with offline parcel-survey evidence. They need to inspect parcel geometry, compare a proposed boundary with active neighbours, review topology evidence, and record a defensible human conclusion without implying statutory registration authority.

### Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a calibrated analysis instrument: calm under routine use, explicit about uncertainty, and rigorous when actions are irreversible.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light industrial land-governance console with tinted neutral surfaces, strong accessible contrast, compact information density, and a vivid but limited signal palette. Parcel geometry and topology evidence are the primary visual signature. Avoid gradients, oversized headings, decorative cards, excessive rounding, generic dashboard tiles, and dark neon styling.

### Design Principles

1. Make parcel geometry and its topology evidence the visual center of review workflows.
2. Keep repeated operational actions compact, predictable, and keyboard accessible.
3. Use color only for meaning; pair every status color with text or shape.
4. Preserve critical controls on narrow screens by reflowing instead of hiding them.
5. Expose uncertainty, permissions, and irreversible state transitions before confirmation.
